Motorhorde Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026. Halvrox LLC, 1309 Coffeen Ave Ste 1200, Sheridan, WY 82801-5777, USA. Contact: v.mavrodi@halvrox.com.

This policy explains what information the Motorhorde app ("the game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Motorhorde is published by Halvrox LLC ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You never create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • The game sends gameplay and diagnostic data to GameAnalytics so we can fix crashes and balance the game.
  • The game can show optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. Ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; otherwise they are non-personalised.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.

Information the game collects

  • Device identifiers: Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV); the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Why: Analytics, crash reports, serving and measuring ads, app functionality.
  • User ID: The identifier the analytics service files your install's data under (the IDFV above). Why: Analytics, app functionality.
  • Gameplay and product interaction: Levels played, runs started and finished, upgrades chosen, buttons and screens used, tutorial steps. Why: Analytics, game balance.
  • Purchase history: Which item was bought and its price (never your payment details). Why: Analytics, delivering what you bought.
  • Advertising data: Which ads were shown, whether a rewarded ad was watched. Why: Serving ads, analytics.
  • Coarse location: Approximate region derived from your IP address by the ad and analytics services (not GPS). Why: Serving ads, analytics.
  • Diagnostics: Crash reports, performance data (frame rate, memory), device model, OS and app version. Why: Fixing crashes and performance problems.

The game does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or health data.

Service providers

GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS) receives the gameplay, purchase and diagnostic data above. See the GameAnalytics privacy policy.

Google AdMob (Google LLC) shows the optional rewarded ads and receives device identifiers, IP-derived coarse location and ad interaction data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Apple processes all in-app purchases under the Apple privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation of the transaction.

Tracking and your choice

After your first finished run the game explains why it asks, and then iOS may show the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use your advertising identifier to show personalised ads and measure them; this counts as "tracking" under Apple's rules. If you do not, ads are non-personalised and the IDFA is not available to us or our providers. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The game, its rewards and its purchases work the same either way.

Ads are optional

Every ad in Motorhorde is a rewarded video you choose to watch (free cores in the shop, continuing a lost run, doubling a victory reward). The game never shows an ad you did not ask for.

Children

Motorhorde is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and want their data removed, contact us.

How long data is kept

Gameplay and diagnostic data is kept only as long as it is useful for the purposes above and according to the retention settings of our providers, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. Because the game has no accounts, data is tied to device identifiers; write to v.mavrodi@halvrox.com and we will help you identify and act on data linked to your device. You can also limit tracking at any time in iOS Settings as described above.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union, under the safeguards described in their own policies.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be noted in the game's release notes.
