Motorhorde

A survivors-style action game where you don't run from the zombie horde — you drive straight through it. Every level-up bolts new hardware onto your armored car: gun pods, missiles, a tesla coil, a flamethrower, orbiting blades. Bosses, a gate to break at the end of every level, and upgrades you can see on the car.

  • Controllers DualSense, Xbox, MFi
