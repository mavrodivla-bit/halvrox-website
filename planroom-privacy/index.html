Planroom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Planroom is published by HALVROX. This policy explains what the app does with your data. It is short because the app collects very little.

No account, no profile

Planroom has no sign-up and no user accounts. We do not know who you are, and we hold no profile about you.

What stays on your device

Everything the app produces is stored locally: 3D scans, room dimensions, floor plans, estimates, generated designs, your company details and logo for PDF letterheads. None of it is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your device, and when

  • Photos, only at the moment you ask for a design. The photo is sent through a proxy server we operate on Cloudflare Workers to OpenAI, which generates the image and returns it. The proxy exists so that our API key never ships inside the app; it does not store your photos. OpenAI does not use content submitted through its API to train its models, and retains it only for a limited period for abuse monitoring, after which it is deleted. If you never request a design, no photo ever leaves your device.
  • Speech, only while the microphone button is held. Dictation of dimensions uses Apple's speech recognition. It runs on your device whenever the device supports it for your language; otherwise the audio is processed on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We receive only the fact that a subscription is active. We never see your name, card details or billing address.
  • Advertising measurement (Meta). The app includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK so we can tell whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram lead to installs. It sends Meta app events such as app launches, the start of a free trial and subscription purchases. We do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not ask for permission to track you, and the data is not linked to your identity. Meta processes it under its own privacy policy.
  • Product analytics (Google Firebase). To understand which features people use and where they get stuck, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example, finishing onboarding, completing a scan, opening an estimate, exporting a PDF, seeing the subscription screen and generating a design. Firebase uses a random app-instance identifier and derives an approximate region from the IP address; it does not receive your photos, scans, name or contacts. Advertising identifier (IDFA) collection and ad personalisation are turned off. Google processes this data under its privacy policy.

What we do not do

No advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites, no precise location, no selling or sharing of data with anyone beyond the processing described above.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to scan the room and to take photos for design.
  • Motion — the LiDAR scan needs device movement data to build an accurate model.
  • Photo library — to let you pick an existing photo instead of taking a new one.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — to dictate dimensions instead of typing them.

Each permission is optional. Declining one disables the matching feature and nothing else.

Children

Planroom is not directed at children and is not designed for use by them.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you already have full control of it: deleting the app erases everything. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom and want to exercise a right of access, correction or erasure regarding anything we might hold, write to v.mavrodi@halvrox.com and we will respond within 30 days. The legal basis for the processing described above is performance of our contract with you — providing the feature you asked for.

International transfers

OpenAI processes images on servers in the United States. Transfers rely on the standard contractual clauses in OpenAI's data processing agreement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
