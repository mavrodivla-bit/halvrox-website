Magnet Sort — mobile game privacy supplement

Effective date and last updated: October 7, 2026.

This supplement describes the current iOS version of Magnet Sort, provided by HALVROX LLC. It covers the game separately from the website, newsletter and contact forms described above.

For information processed through Magnet Sort, this supplement takes precedence over the website-only sections above. HALVROX LLC is responsible for the game data processing described here; Apple and Google also process information under their own privacy policies.

Game progress and local records

You do not need a game account. Magnet Sort saves your level, puzzle state, coins, lives, inventory, rewards, workshop progress and settings on your device. It also keeps local records of gameplay, purchases and reward delivery to operate the game and prevent duplicate grants.

Local gameplay logs include randomly generated session and event identifiers, timestamps, app version, level attempts, game actions, economy changes and advertising outcomes. From version 1.0.2, the same gameplay events are also sent to Google Analytics for Firebase, as described in "Game analytics" below. Advertising services process data separately as explained below.

Device backups may include app data according to your device and Apple backup settings. The game does not provide its own account-based cloud save service.

Game analytics

From version 1.0.2, Magnet Sort uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC, to understand how the game is played: which levels are too hard, how the in-game economy works, and whether optional purchases and rewarded videos work correctly. It receives levels started, won and lost; use of helpers such as undo, shuffle and extra tray; rewarded videos offered and watched, including estimated ad revenue; Apple-verified in-app purchases (product, price and currency); and app version, device model, operating system, an approximate country derived from the IP address, and an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase. We do not send your name, email address, contacts or payment card details. If you allow tracking in Apple's "Allow Tracking" dialog, Firebase may also use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to measure advertising; if you do not, it is not used. Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months. See Firebase's privacy information.

Advertising and privacy choices

Magnet Sort uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Ads can be requested and loaded in advance, after the consent system permits requests; processing is not limited to the moment you choose to watch a video.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app or device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic and performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, service operation and improvement. Processing depends on the SDK configuration, applicable requirements and your privacy choices. See Google's advertising information and privacy policy.

Advertising information may also be shared with advertising partners that provide or measure ads through Google's services. Where the consent message is shown, its partner list identifies participating companies and explains their purposes and privacy policies. The game does not request access to your device's precise GPS location.

Before rewarded advertising starts, the game asks for Apple's permission to access the advertising identifier (IDFA) through the "Allow Tracking" dialog. If you decline, the IDFA is not used and ads are not personalised with it; advertising services may still process other identifiers and technical data as described above. You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings.

Where an advertising privacy message is required, the game presents it before requesting ads as required by the consent system. When available, you can reopen the advertising privacy choices from the game's settings. Changing a choice affects subsequent processing; it does not undo processing already performed.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. HALVROX does not receive your payment card details through the game. The app uses Apple-verified purchase information, including product and transaction identifiers and entitlement status, to deliver purchases, restore eligible purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. Its delivery records are stored locally. Apple's processing is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Contacting us

If you contact HALVROX, we receive the contact details and information you choose to send. We use these to respond, investigate the request and maintain necessary support records. Do not send payment card details or passwords. The game does not automatically attach your saved game or local logs to an email.

HALVROX LLC can be contacted at v.mavrodi@halvrox.com, or 1309 Coffeen Avenue STE 1200, Sheridan, Wyoming 82801, United States.

Retention and your choices

The current version retains its local save and logs in the app's storage. Deleting the app removes that local app storage; restoring a device backup may restore it. Deleting the game does not delete purchase records held by Apple or information already processed by advertising or analytics providers.

You can contact us about access, correction or deletion of information HALVROX holds about you and other privacy rights available in your location. HALVROX cannot remotely retrieve or delete a save that exists only on your device. Requests relating to information held independently by Apple or Google can also be directed to those providers using their privacy controls.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve your request, handle related follow-up or disputes, and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. The length depends on the nature of the request and those obligations. Apple, Google and other advertising partners set retention periods for information they hold under their own policies.

Children and younger players

Magnet Sort is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support requests. If you believe a child has provided personal information to HALVROX, contact v.mavrodi@halvrox.com so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Age and parental-consent requirements can differ by location; a store age rating does not itself provide consent for advertising data processing.

European privacy rights and legal grounds

Where European Economic Area, UK or Swiss data protection law applies, the grounds for our processing depend on the purpose: providing the game and delivering requested purchases; our legitimate interests in responding to support requests, operating the game securely, preventing duplicate or fraudulent rewards and understanding how the game is played to improve it; compliance with applicable legal obligations; and consent for advertising storage or processing where required. Consent choices and the advertising partners' stated grounds are available in the advertising privacy message.

You may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive a portable copy of your personal information, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. Contact v.mavrodi@halvrox.com to exercise rights concerning information HALVROX holds. Providing support contact details is voluntary; without the information needed to understand your request, we may be unable to resolve it.

US state privacy choices

Depending on your state and applicable law, you may have rights to know or access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, and to opt out of its sale, sharing or use for targeted advertising. Advertising-related disclosures of identifiers and activity information described above may count as sale or sharing under these laws even without a payment for the information. Use the game's advertising privacy choices, where available, to make advertising opt-out choices, or contact v.mavrodi@halvrox.com for requests concerning HALVROX. International processing and security

HALVROX LLC is based in the United States. Information you send to us and information processed by advertising, analytics or payment services may be processed outside your country. Those countries may have different privacy laws. Google's arrangements for relevant transfers, including its adequacy frameworks and standard contractual clauses, are explained in its data transfer information. Apple describes its international processing in its privacy policy. These providers' arrangements do not mean HALVROX itself is certified under those frameworks. Contact us for information about the handling of personal information you submit directly to HALVROX.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. The game does not upload its local gameplay records to servers operated by HALVROX; gameplay statistics are processed by Google Analytics for Firebase as described above.
