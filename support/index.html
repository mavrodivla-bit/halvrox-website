Motorhorde Support

Write to v.mavrodi@halvrox.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the game).

I bought something and don't see it

Open the Shop or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. The Starter Pack and the Core Doubler come back on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Cores and rerolls you already spent cannot be restored.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored on your device and is removed if the app is deleted. Restore Purchases returns the Starter Pack and the Core Doubler.

Ads and tracking

Ads are optional rewarded videos. To change whether apps may track you, go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the privacy policy.
