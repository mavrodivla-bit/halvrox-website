Haystacked — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026. Developer: Halvrox L.L.C.

The short version

We, the developer, do not collect or store any personal data, and there are no accounts, analytics or tracking. On iPhone and iPad you can choose to watch an optional ad for a reward; those ads come from Google AdMob, which may process device data as described below. Coin packs are bought through Apple.

What stays on your device

Your saved games and settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple may keep a copy of your saved games in your private iCloud storage so you can continue on another device. We cannot see it.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple shows your best times on leaderboards and your achievements. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive no personal data from it.

Optional rewarded ads (iPhone and iPad)

An ad is shown only when you tap "Watch an ad". The ad is served by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers, your approximate location from your IP address and information about how you interact with the ad, to show and measure it. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first. We do not ask iOS for permission to track you. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Coin packs are processed by Apple. We receive no payment or personal details.

Children

We collect no data from anyone, including children. The game is not directed at children under 13.
